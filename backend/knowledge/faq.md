# Hostel Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: How do I get Wi-Fi access in my hostel room?
**A:** Connect to the `Campus_Hostel_5G` or `Campus_Hostel_2.4G` Wi-Fi SSID. A captive portal page will appear. Log in using your Student Roll Number and the default password provided during admission. If the captive portal does not open, navigate to `http://10.0.0.1` or register a complaint under the "Wi-Fi" category in AIVA.

### Q2: What should I do if my room has an electrical or water failure?
**A:** File an immediate complaint using AIVA via the Web Portal or Telegram (`/complaint`). Select either "Electricity" or "Water Supply", specify your Block and Room Number, and add a brief description. Maintenance technicians inspect emergency issues between 08:00 AM and 08:00 PM.

### Q3: What is the procedure for changing rooms?
**A:** Room change requests are entertained only at the end of each semester. Download the Room Change Request Form from the student portal, obtain the signature of your current roommate and block warden, and submit it to the Chief Warden's office during the designated window.

### Q4: What happens if I lose my room key?
**A:** Report the lost key to your Block Caretaker immediately. A temporary spare key can be issued for 24 hours against your Student ID. If the key is not found, a lock replacement fee of Rs. 250 must be paid at the hostel accounts desk before a new lock and duplicate keys are installed.

### Q5: Can I keep an electric kettle or iron in my room?
**A:** Electric kettles up to 500W are permitted for boiling water. Electric irons, immersion rods, and induction cookers are strictly prohibited due to fire safety hazards and load limitations. A designated ironing room is available on the 1st floor of each block.

### Q6: What are the hostel gate timings and curfew?
**A:** The main hostel gate opens at 06:00 AM and closes at 10:00 PM. Residents must be inside their blocks by 10:30 PM. For late entry due to academic projects or library study, obtain a digital pass signed by your department head by 05:00 PM.

### Q7: How can I register a night-out or weekend leave?
**A:** Submit a Night-Out Application on the university portal at least 24 hours prior to departure. An automated SMS/email confirmation is dispatched to your registered parent/guardian. Once approved by the Assistant Warden, show the digital QR gate pass at the security checkpoint.

### Q8: What should I do if I find or lose an item in the hostel?
**A:** Go to the "Lost & Found" section in the AIVA Web Portal. You can report a lost item or report an item you found with its location, photo, and category. Items turned in to security are logged and can be claimed with proof of ownership.

### Q9: What are the mess dining timings?
**A:**
- Breakfast: 07:30 AM – 09:30 AM
- Lunch: 12:30 PM – 02:30 PM
- Evening Tea & Snacks: 05:00 PM – 06:30 PM
- Dinner: 07:30 PM – 09:30 PM

### Q10: How do I request human staff intervention if AI cannot solve my issue?
**A:** In the AIVA web chat, click the **"Request Human Help"** button at any time. If you are using Telegram, send the `/human` command. Your conversation will be flagged for hostel staff and an assistant warden will review and reply directly.

### Q11: How do I link my Telegram bot to my web account?
**A:**
1. Log in to the AIVA Web Portal with Google.
2. Go to Settings or Profile and click "Link Telegram Account" to generate an 8-character verification code.
3. Open the Telegram bot and send: `/link YOUR_CODE`.
4. Your Telegram conversations and complaints will automatically sync with your web profile.

### Q12: Are day visitors or guests allowed in hostel rooms?
**A:** No. Day visitors (parents and guardians) are only allowed in the ground floor visitor lounge between 09:00 AM and 06:00 PM. No visitor is allowed inside student rooms.

### Q13: Where are the washing machines located?
**A:** Automated laundry machines are located in the Ground Floor Utility Area of each block. They operate from 06:00 AM to 10:00 PM on a first-come, first-served basis.

### Q14: What is the protocol during a medical emergency at night?
**A:** Contact the on-duty resident caretaker or security guard immediately. The 24/7 campus health center ambulance can be dispatched by dialing Extension 1008 or +91-11-2345-6708. First aid kits are available with the security desk on the ground floor.

### Q15: How can I track the progress of a complaint I submitted?
**A:** On the web dashboard, visit the "Complaints" tab to view live status badges (Submitted, Acknowledged, In Progress, Resolved). On Telegram, send `/status <REFERENCE_NUMBER>` (e.g., `/status AIVA-20261031-1234`).
