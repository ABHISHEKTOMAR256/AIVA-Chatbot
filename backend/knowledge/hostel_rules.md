# University Hostel Rules and Regulations

## 1. General Conduct and Discipline
All residents of the university hostel are expected to maintain high standards of discipline, decorum, and mutual respect. Any behavior that disrupts peace, harms fellow residents, or damages university property is strictly prohibited. Ragging in any form is a criminal offense under national law and results in immediate expulsion and police reporting.

## 2. Check-in, Check-out, and Hostel Timings
- **Hostel Main Gate:** Opens at 06:00 AM and closes strictly at 10:00 PM every night.
- **Night Curfew:** Residents must be inside their respective blocks by 10:30 PM.
- **Late Entry:** Late entry between 10:00 PM and 11:00 PM requires explicit permission from the Assistant Warden. Repeated late entries without prior leave approval lead to disciplinary action and parent notification.
- **Night Out / Leave Pass:** Any student intending to stay out overnight must submit an online gate pass application through the hostel portal at least 24 hours in advance, verified by their guardian.

## 3. Room Allocation and Maintenance
- Rooms are allocated at the beginning of each academic session by the Hostel Administration. Unauthorized room swapping or subletting is strictly forbidden.
- Residents are provided with a cot, study table, chair, and wardrobe. Any pre-existing damage must be reported within 48 hours of check-in.
- Students are responsible for the daily cleanliness of their rooms. Deep cleaning of corridors and common restrooms is carried out daily by housekeeping staff between 09:00 AM and 01:00 PM.
- Painting walls, using nails, or pasting stickers that deface room surfaces is not permitted.

## 4. Electrical Appliances and Safety
- **Permitted Appliances:** Mobile chargers, laptops, table lamps, and electric kettles (up to 500W).
- **Prohibited Appliances:** Immersion water rods, electric heaters, induction stoves, toasters, and air conditioners. Any unauthorized heating appliance found during inspection will be confiscated.
- Turn off all lights, fans, and appliances before leaving the room to conserve energy.

## 5. Wi-Fi and Internet Usage
- High-speed campus Wi-Fi is available 24/7 across all hostel blocks.
- Each student is issued individual credentials linked to their student registration ID.
- Network usage is subject to the university's Acceptable IT Usage Policy. Torrenting, cyber-bullying, and accessing restricted domains are tracked and prohibited.
- For connectivity outages or slow speeds, register a complaint under the "Wi-Fi" category in the AIVA portal with your room and block number.

## 6. Mess and Dining Services
- **Breakfast:** 07:30 AM – 09:30 AM
- **Lunch:** 12:30 PM – 02:30 PM
- **Evening Snacks:** 05:00 PM – 06:30 PM
- **Dinner:** 07:30 PM – 09:30 PM
- Taking mess utensils or food plates to hostel rooms is strictly prohibited.
- A student mess committee reviews the food menu on a monthly basis.

## 7. Visitor and Guest Policy
- Day visitors (parents and immediate family members) are allowed only in the designated visitor lounge between 09:00 AM and 06:00 PM.
- Visitors are not allowed inside student rooms under any circumstances.
- Overnight stay for male guests in female blocks or female guests in male blocks is strictly prohibited.

## 8. Laundry and Washing Facilities
- Automated coin/card-operated washing machines are located on the ground floor of each block.
- Washing facilities are open from 06:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
- Clothes must be dried only in designated drying yards on the terrace or utility balconies.

## 9. Complaint Resolution Mechanism
- Any infrastructure, electrical, plumbing, or hygiene issues must be registered immediately through the **AIVA Hostel Helpdesk** (via Web Portal or Telegram).
- Standard resolution timelines:
  - Electricity & Wi-Fi: within 4 to 8 working hours.
  - Plumbing & Water: within 6 to 12 working hours.
  - Carpentry & Civil Maintenance: within 24 to 48 working hours.
- If an issue remains unresolved beyond the standard SLA, use the "Request Human Help" option in AIVA to escalate to the Assistant Warden.
